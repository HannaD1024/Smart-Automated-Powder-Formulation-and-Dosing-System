# Smart-Automated-Powder-Formulation-and-Dosing-System  
The goal of this project is to design and build a compact automated system capable of accurately dispensing and combining three powders with different particle sizes, densities, colors, and flow characteristics  

# Constraints & Considerations  
Technical:   
•	Solution shall mix 3 colored powders to create a new mixed color  
•	Resulting mixture color shall be accurate to the desired input  
•	Solution shall be easy to transport  
•	Solution shall be modular  
•	Input powder containers shall be removable  
•	Output powder container shall be removable  
•	Solution shall be powered by 12V  
•	Solution may be battery powered   
•	Final mixture amount shall not exceed output containers  
•	Solution shall approximately fit in a 10”x5” cylindrical container	 

Budget:   
•	Prototype shall cost less than $2000  
Timeline:  
•	Project shall be completed by May 2027  
Regulatory / safety / ethical:  
•	The product shall be food safe.
